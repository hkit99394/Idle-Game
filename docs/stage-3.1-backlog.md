# Stage 3.1 Backlog: District Heat Contract And Pacing Hardening

## Current Status

Stage 3.1 is the active post-Intrusion planning and hardening milestone. [Archived Stage 3.0 Backlog](archive/stage-3.0-backlog.md) proved the first neon-native mechanic through Intrusion without save, export, event, taxonomy, or broad balance churn.

This stage prepares **District Heat** as the preferred second Path of Neon prototype, but it should not ship live heat penalties, persisted heat, or UI promises before the contract and pacing evidence are ready. District Heat touches offline farming, assignments, rewards, region UI, save posture, and balance gates, so the first milestone should make those boundaries explicit.

Slices 97.1, 97.2, and 97.3 are complete. [District Heat Contract](district-heat-contract.md) selects report-only projection as the first implementation path, defers derived or persisted live heat until later evidence justifies it, records the offline/assignment pacing baseline, and classifies known balance debt before heat can modify live rewards or risk.

## Stage Theme

Make route choice and offline farming feel like cyber-underworld pressure without destabilizing the economy. Stage 3.1 should answer what District Heat is, where it lives, how it changes reports first, and what evidence is required before it becomes a player-facing mechanic.

The milestone should turn District Heat from a theme-bible candidate into an implementation-ready contract. It should start with report-only or documentation-only proof and keep existing budget debt visible.

## Source Contracts And Carry-Forward Decisions

- [Path Of Neon Theme Bible](path-of-neon-theme-bible.md) names District Heat as the preferred second neon-native system after Cognitive Intrusion.
- [Path Of Neon Terminology Map](path-of-neon-terminology-map.md) defines District Heat as attention generated by repeated or risky route operations.
- [Path Of Neon Retheme Migration Plan](retheme-migration-plan.md) recommends documenting a heat contract and adding report-only fields before changing rewards.
- [District Heat Contract](district-heat-contract.md) selects report-only projection first, forbids Stage 3.1 save/UI/reward churn, and defines promotion gates for later live mechanics.
- [Progression Pacing Roadmap](progression-pacing-roadmap.md) warns that offline farming currently uses a fixed clear-time assumption and needs active/offline parity checks before serious economy tuning.
- [Content Pipeline Inventory](content-pipeline-inventory.md), [Balance Budget Gates](balance-budget-gates.md), configured `balanceTargets`, and `npm run simulate` remain the release authority for known Black Iron Foundry and Redline Outpost debt.
- [Web UI Architecture](web-ui-architecture.md) keeps deferred mechanic terms out of live UI until the corresponding system exists.
- Stage 3.0 keep decisions still apply: do not remove tactic comparison legacy damage columns, engine-level `outerDamage` / `innerDamage`, static taxonomy keys, or compatibility aliases as part of District Heat.

## Scope

- A District Heat contract that chooses report-only, derived, or persisted state for the first implementation path.
- Offline farming and assignment pacing preflight, including active/offline reward parity risk.
- Known balance debt review before heat can modify rewards or risk.
- Report-only heat projection design, and optionally the smallest report-only implementation if the contract is clear.
- Save, cloud-save, export, and UI boundary decisions before any live heat mechanic.
- Docs updates that mark Stage 3.1 as active and keep District Heat out of player-facing UI until implemented.

## Non-Goals

- No live District Heat UI, player-facing heat meter, reward penalty, or route modifier before the contract and report-only gate pass.
- No persisted heat field, save-version bump, cloud-save envelope change, or migration unless a dedicated slice explicitly approves it.
- No Trace, Firewall, Calibration Debt, hostile Intrusion expansion, AI Raid, augment loadout, protocol deck, network operation, or countermeasure economy implementation.
- No new region or broad content expansion.
- No broad Black Iron Foundry, Redline Outpost, or economy retune hidden inside heat prep.
- No export schema bump unless a slice explicitly decides report-only heat requires one.

## Exit Criteria

- A District Heat contract exists and defines state model, gain/decay rules, caps, affected surfaces, save posture, UI posture, report posture, and promotion gates.
- Offline farming and assignment pacing risks are documented with concrete code/test/report owners.
- Known Black Iron Foundry and Redline Outpost debt is classified as blocker, deferred debt, or explicit future tuning before heat changes economy pressure.
- Report-only heat projection has a decided schema or design and does not silently change live rewards.
- Active docs point to Stage 3.1 while archived Stage 3.0 remains the closed Intrusion record.
- Deferred terms such as Trace, Firewall, Calibration Debt, and AI Raid stay out of live UI.

## Slice 97.2: Offline And Pacing Baseline Audit

Prove the current offline and pacing assumptions before heat changes route rewards or pressure.

### Tasks

- Audit `core/offline/offlineRewards.ts`, `core/offline/assignmentRewards.ts`, `core/progression/types.ts`, `core/progression/stages.ts`, `web/state/viewModels/offline.ts`, and related tests.
- Document the fixed offline clear-time assumption and whether Stage 3.1 should replace it, report it, or leave it deferred.
- Compare active simulated clear times against offline reward rates for representative farm stages.
- Identify where assignment rewards should or should not participate in heat gain.
- Add or update focused tests only if the audit finds an existing mismatch that should be locked before heat work.

### Implementation Notes

- Audited `core/offline/offlineRewards.ts`, `core/offline/assignmentRewards.ts`, `core/progression/types.ts`, `core/progression/stages.ts`, `web/state/viewModels/offline.ts`, `tests/offline/offlineRewards.test.ts`, `tests/offline/assignmentRewards.test.ts`, and `data/assignments.json`.
- Recorded the current offline farm formula in [District Heat Contract](district-heat-contract.md): default `estimatedClearTimeSeconds: 10`, `minimumClearTimeSeconds: 5`, and `offlineEfficiency: 0.6`, with no stage-specific active or target clear time.
- Compared active simulator clear times against default offline reward rate for current recommended farm routes. Offline currently exceeds active reward rate on `greenline_approach_8` (`1.51x`), `black_iron_foundry_6` (`2.70x`), `lotus_clinic_6` (`2.45x`), and `redline_outpost_6` (`2.16x`); `veil_district_5` is near parity (`0.97x`).
- Decision: Stage 3.1 should report the parity risk before replacing the offline formula. District Heat cannot modify live offline rewards until the parity decision is explicit in a later slice.
- Decision: assignments may contribute report-only projected heat only when `rewardProfile.mapRegionId` exists; assignments without a district target contribute no projected heat for now.
- No runtime behavior or tests changed because the audit found documented current behavior, not an implementation mismatch.

### Acceptance

- The backlog or contract records the current active/offline parity risk with concrete owner files.
- District Heat cannot change offline rewards until the parity decision is explicit.
- Assignment participation is decided or explicitly deferred.
